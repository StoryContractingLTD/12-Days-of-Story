How to play

  • A new door opens at 8am each working day. Each puzzle takes about 2–3 minutes.
  • Finish a game and your score saves to the leaderboard automatically. Use the same name each day so your points add up. Only your first go each day counts.
  • Stuck on an escape room? Hints are there if you need them.
  • Keep your escape-room codes from Days 3 and 7. You'll need them on Day 11.
  • Door 12 is the Grand Finale: a 12-question Christmas quiz, open all day on 16 December.

How the money comes in

  • Donate to take part: give what you can on the fundraising page, then play as many doors as you like.
  • Honesty system: nobody checks. We trust you, it's Christmas.
  • Not a gamer? The Humbug Door lets you donate without playing.

Every pound goes to our chosen charity. Thank you for taking part!
